Modifications made for PureRefLike: selected the upright female A-pose from the
public package; removed presentation copies and duplicate outline geometry;
split bilateral rigid meshes into left and right parts; reconstructed a semantic
rigid hierarchy, joint pivots, limits, compound elbow/knee controls, IK metadata,
and hand presets; removed texture coordinates; reduced the runtime mesh; and
replaced the materials with three untextured clay/joint/slot roles.
